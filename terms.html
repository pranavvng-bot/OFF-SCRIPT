Terms & conditions

The short, readable version of how we work together. Last updated: September 2026.

Quotes & scope

Every project begins with a written, itemized quote. The quoted price covers the quoted scope only; new requests are quoted separately before work continues. Prices on this website are starting points, not offers.

Payments

Projects are invoiced by milestone — typically an advance to begin, a payment at design approval and the balance at final delivery. Invoices are due within 7 days unless agreed otherwise in writing.

Timelines

We commit to the timeline in your quote, provided feedback and materials arrive on schedule. Delays in feedback extend the timeline by the same duration.

Ownership

On final payment, you own the delivered code, design files and content. We retain the right to display the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Revisions

Each milestone includes two rounds of revisions. Additional rounds are quoted at the rates in your agreement.

Support & warranty

Every launch includes a post-launch support window (2–6 weeks depending on your package) covering bugs and small fixes. Ongoing care is available through a maintenance plan.
