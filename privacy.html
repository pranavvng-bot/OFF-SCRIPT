Legal

Privacy policy

Plain language, because privacy policies should be readable. Last updated: September 2026.

What we collect

When you contact us or submit a project request, we receive the information you give us: your name, email, phone number and project details. We also keep standard technical logs (browser type, pages visited) for site security and performance.

How we use it

Your information is used to reply to you, prepare quotes, deliver projects and keep the client dashboard accurate. We don't sell, rent or trade your data. We don't send marketing emails without your explicit opt-in.

Client files

Files you upload stay with us for the duration of your project and are deleted on request after handover. Project records are visible only to the four studio members.

Cookies & local storage

This site stores your theme preference and (in the preview) your demo client session in your browser's local storage. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it: hello@off-script.studio.
